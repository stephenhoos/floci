Adapted classes: ApacheDockerHttpClient, ApacheDockerHttpClientImpl and
HijackingHttpRequestExecutor, now FlociDockerHttpClient and HijackingHttpRequestExecutor.
Changes include a Floci-owned builder, JBoss Logging, switch expressions, connection
pool validation and eviction, bounded connection leases, transient-I/O retries,
explicit certificate trust and screened outbound HTTP clients.
